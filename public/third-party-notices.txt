OpenNGC deep-sky subset, contours and derived component atlas
Copyright: Mattia Verga / OpenNGC contributors
Source: https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC/tree/be3cd6c5498806a2fbb2f7e0452ef802dbb02be0
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Original outlines traced with Aladin Sky Atlas (CDS, Strasbourg Observatory)
from DSS2 Color. The contour source distributes no original DSS photographic pixels.
Changes: eight-object selection, Chinese labels/aliases, tangent-plane bounds;
component masks and smooth display profiles are qualitative derived models,
not measured spectroscopy or calibrated exposure. The JSON and derived atlas
remain CC BY-SA 4.0 and are available from the preview attribution.
M45 associated stars use the separately credited HYG subset above.
Input and derived file hashes and coordinate conventions: src/data/deep-sky.json.

Deep-sky photographic structure references (M42/M31 only)
M42 credit: ESO/Igor Chekalin
Source: https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso1103a/
Usage: https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/
M31 credit: ÷ 2002 R. Gendler, Photo by R. Gendler
(credit string preserved from the ESA/Hubble/AstroPix publication)
Source: https://esahubble.org/images/heic0512d/
Downloaded from publisher archive: https://www.astropix.org/image/esahubble/heic0512d
Usage: https://esahubble.org/copyright/
Original images: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Modified: AVM/TAN reprojection, resizing, median/morphological star suppression,
black-level and display-tone changes, qualitative colour-component proxies,
elliptical feathering over a faint OpenNGC envelope. No AI processing.
Combined component atlas: CC BY-SA 4.0; original images remain CC BY 4.0.
Original photographs are not bundled. This is structure reference, not measured
spectral flux, validated source astrometry or a camera/exposure simulation.
Full attribution and input/derived hashes: src/data/deep-sky.json.

ESO Milky Way panorama (eso0932a)
Credit: ESO/S. Brunier
Source: https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Usage policy: https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/
Modified: cropped to Galactic latitude +/-22.5 degrees, resized, suppressed
photographic stars/halos, denoised, adjusted black level and longitude seam,
encoded as WebP. No AI processing. Photographic appearance, not astrometry
or calibrated photometry. Source and generated SHA-256 hashes are retained
in src/data/milky-way.json; generation script: scripts/build-milky-way.py.
